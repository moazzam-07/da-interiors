# Kydmah Main Goals

## Objective
Build a premium digital system for Kydmah that helps the business attract customers, manage service orders, assign staff, and improve operational visibility without turning the platform into a heavy ERP.

## Business Requirements
- Present Kydmah as a premium and trustworthy maintenance brand.
- Convert visitors into structured service bookings.
- Give admins a clean control panel for orders, offers, reports, and content.
- Include staff assignment so jobs can be operationally managed.
- Keep customers informed through email notifications across the order lifecycle.
- Support Google review growth as a direct sales lever.

## Product Principles
- Keep the experience premium, simple, and conversion-focused.
- Prioritize practical operations over enterprise complexity.
- Build modularly so the website, booking system, and admin panel can evolve separately.
- Make the system usable by a small operational team without heavy training.

## Future Ambitions
- Introduce richer customer retention and repeat-service campaigns.
- Add WhatsApp or SMS communication later if needed.
- Improve branch-level reporting and operational analytics.
- Expand into deeper CRM, staff coordination, or other business modules only when justified.
